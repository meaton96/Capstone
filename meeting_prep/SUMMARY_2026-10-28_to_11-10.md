## Results
| experiment | result |
|---|---|
| `rq3-alpha-transport`, `eval-alpha-transport-size` (10-28/29) | α law with a transport term (travel / op time per AGV) plus a gate that fixes the pair above 50 machines. Never worse than MDD-PTECT on six floors; −19.0% at 200 machines, −14.3% at 100 with a short fleet. Holds at 75 machines but not at 150, so it is reported as a fit to these floors. |
| `eval-alpha-only-size` (10-28) | With the pair fixed, the α offsets alone reach −4.7% at 200 machines (full policy −0.3%). The pair heads cost 2-6 pts on large floors, which is why the gate exists. |
| `rq3-alpha-online`, `eval-alpha-online` (10-30) | α set from the AGVs on duty fits better (held-out regret 1.2% vs 3.3%) but the policy wins on only 2 of 6 floors. Closed; the static law stays. |
| `eval-alpha-unity` (11-01) | Unity check passes. B2 −0.2% (n.s.; twin −1.0%). 100 machines −4.8% [−7.5, −2.3] (twin −4.3%). The twin's ranking holds although Unity adds +50% tardiness at 100 machines. |
| `eval-ft` 100-machine arm (10-28) | 24 fine-tune updates gain 1.05 pts on 100 machines (p 0.01) and keep B2, but get only 40% of the way to a policy trained at 100 machines. Supports "train at the largest deployment size". |
| `dev-failure-resume`, `rq4-mfail-ccal-resume`, `agv-study-fail-resume` (10-28/30) | Preempt-resume failures: Unity and twin match exactly (27/27 cells). With a matched due-date allowance the rule ranking is unchanged by failures (τ 1.00). AGV-study failure cells: release protocol clean, knee at 10 AGVs. |
| `rq2-replan-axes` axis 1, time scale (10-29) | Scaling processing time or regime length moves the best interval Δ* (0.36-1.29 × mean op time). B2's 5 min keeps only 77-83% of the gain elsewhere. |
| axis 6, floor size and fleet (11-04) | Re-planning beats plan-once by 4.6-10.2 pts at every size up to 100 machines. |
| axis 2, load × due-date tightness (11-08) | Gains 3.9-19.5 pts. A tuned interval carries only as far as its plateau reaches. Re-planning every 60 s keeps ≥ 92% of the best gain at all 35 conditions. |
| fit across axes 1, 2, 6 (11-09) | No equation beats a constant. 0.36 × mean op time keeps ≥ 85% of the best gain at 34 of 35 held-out conditions, at half the compute of 60 s. Each doubling past Δ* costs about 10 pts. From about 100 machines compute binds: one re-plan takes about half the interval. |

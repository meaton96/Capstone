\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Manages the lifecycle and retrieval of the \doxylink{namespace_assets_1_1_scripts_1_1_simulation_1_1_a_g_v}{AGV} fleet. 

Serves as a centralized container and factory. The {\ttfamily \doxylink{class_assets_1_1_scripts_1_1_simulation_1_1_simulation_bridge}{Simulation\+Bridge}} orchestrator queries this pool to identify and dispatch available units based on their current operational state.
